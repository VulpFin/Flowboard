What this page checks

That the application is serving this page, and that the database answers a query. It reports a summary on purpose — detailed readiness diagnostics stay behind the operator's tooling rather than being published to anyone who asks.

What it cannot tell you

Whether your AI provider or calendar provider is having an outage. Those calls go out with your credentials to a third party, so a failure there shows up as an error on the action you tried, not here — check the provider's own status page. The same goes for mail delivery delays.

Where interruptions get announced

Planned work and anything notable that broke is written up in the changelog. For anything else, support reaches a person and works even when you cannot sign in.
