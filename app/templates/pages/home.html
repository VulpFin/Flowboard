Calendars alongside planning

You may connect a calendar provider so Flowboard can show available calendars and busy availability alongside your planning data. When you choose to add a dated task to a connected calendar, Flowboard can create and update that event there.

Integrations on your terms

Connect only what helps

Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 integrations are optional. Calendar access helps Flowboard avoid scheduling work over meetings and lets you place a Flowboard task on a calendar you select.

Read about third-party services

AI with your credentials

You choose the provider

Flowboard's assistant uses an AI provider credential you configure. It proposes changes for review; it does not apply them on its own. Provider data handling terms apply to requests sent to that provider.

Read the privacy policy

Privacy and control

Connections are optional, credentials are encrypted at rest, and you can disconnect a provider whenever you choose. Flowboard does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
