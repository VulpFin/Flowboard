Google Calendar OAuth Review Guide

This guide is for a normal Flowboard reviewer account supplied separately by the Flowboard administrator. Reviewer credentials are never published here.

  1. Visit Flowboard sign in and sign in with the supplied local Flowboard account.
  2. Open Settings → Calendars & integrations.
  3. Select Connect Google Calendar, review the Flowboard explanation, and choose Continue to Google.
  4. Complete Google's consent flow and return to Flowboard.
  5. Use Refresh calendars to show the calendars Flowboard can use, then use Show availability to display busy time Flowboard reads for scheduling.
  6. Open a board, add a dated task, choose the Google connection, and select + Calendar. Verify the event in Google Calendar.
  7. Edit the dated task and verify its Google Calendar event updates. Removing the task's due date removes the linked event; disconnecting the account does not delete pre-existing Google events.

Flowboard requests calendar.readonly and calendar.events. See Third-Party Services and the Privacy Policy for scope and data-use details.
